# Demo corpus — sources & licenses

Sample documents bundled for the public demo. Each is free to redistribute;
source URL + license basis noted below. Deliberately varied in kind (a literary
text, a government report with tables/figures, a technical spec) so a new
visitor can explore the system's range, not just academic-paper QA.

- **alice_in_wonderland_gutenberg11.txt** — *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*
  (Lewis Carroll). Source: Project Gutenberg eBook #11,
  <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/11/11-0.txt>. **Public domain** in the US
  (Project Gutenberg; the work long predates the 1929 cutoff).

- **cdc_nchs_databrief_529.pdf** — "Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease in
  Adults Age 18 and Over" (NCHS Data Brief No. 529, May 2025). Source: CDC /
  National Center for Health Statistics,
  <https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db529.pdf>. **Public domain** — a
  work of the US federal government under 17 U.S.C. § 105. Contains data tables
  (pp. 7–8) and figures, exercising the table/figure viewers.

- **semantic_versioning_spec.md** — Semantic Versioning 2.0.0 (Tom
  Preston-Werner). Source:
  <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/semver/semver/master/semver.md>. Licensed
  **CC BY 3.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>), as stated in
  the document's License section.
